10 - THE 16 FACTS TO REMEMBER
=============================
Every number below is computed in analysis.py and detailed in the other fact files.

 1. SCALE: 99,441 orders from Sep 2016 to Oct 2018, 96,096 unique customers, 3,053 active sellers, GMV of R$ 13,494,401 (item prices) plus R$ 2,241,126 in freight.

 2. GROWTH: Jan-Aug 2018 had 135% more orders and 138% more GMV than Jan-Aug 2017. Orders plateaued at about 6-7K per month through 2018.

 3. VOLUME-DRIVEN: baskets did not grow - order value and items per order stayed flat. Growth = more orders.

 4. BLACK FRIDAY: 1,176 orders on 24 Nov 2017, the single biggest day in the dataset.

 5. CONCENTRATION (geography): São Paulo is 42% of orders; SP+RJ+MG are 66%.

 6. RETENTION: only 3.1% of customers ever ordered twice - this is a one-off-purchase marketplace.

 7. CATEGORIES: 'health_beauty' is #1 at 9.3% of GMV; the top 10 categories are 62% of GMV.

 8. PAYMENTS: credit cards are 78% of money paid, boleto 18%; 67% of card payments use 2+ instalments.

 9. SELLER CONCENTRATION: the top 10% of sellers earn 67% of GMV (Gini 0.79).

10. DELIVERY: average 12.6 days (median 10.2); customers were promised 23.7 days, i.e. goods arrive ~12 days earlier than promised.

11. LATE RATE: 6.8% of delivered orders arrive after the estimated date; worst month was 2018-03 at 19.0%.

12. GEOGRAPHY OF WAIT: 64% of items cross state lines (15.0 days vs 7.9 days same-state); AL waits 24 days vs 9 in SP.

13. SATISFACTION: average review 4.09/5, 58% give 5 stars, 15% give 1-2 stars.

14. THE MEASURABLE DRIVER: on-time orders average 4.29 stars, late orders 2.27; 8+ days late = 1.70 stars. Late orders are 54% 1-star vs 7% for on-time.

15. FUNNEL: 8,000 qualified leads -> 842 won (10.5%), median 15 days to close; best identified channel 'paid search' (12.3%), weakest 'email' (3.0%).

16. HIDDEN RISK: the 1,263 orders with items from 2+ sellers average only 2.86 stars vs 4.13 for single-seller orders.


Suggested actions the data supports
-----------------------------------
  1. Fix the last mile first: late delivery is the biggest controllable cause of 1-star reviews.
  2. Grow repeat purchase: with <4% repeaters, small retention gains are worth more than extra ad spend.
  3. Recruit sellers outside São Paulo to shorten distance, delivery time and freight for the other states.
  4. Watch seller quality: a small number of sellers drive most revenue, so their service level sets the platform's reputation.
  5. Prepare for peaks: lateness jumped in Nov 2017 (Black Friday) and Feb-Mar 2018 - add carrier capacity before promotional events and investigate the early-2018 spike.
  6. Investigate multi-seller orders: they are rare (1.3%) but score about 1.3 stars lower.
  7. Put more recruiting effort into the channels that convert best and re-examine the weakest ones.
