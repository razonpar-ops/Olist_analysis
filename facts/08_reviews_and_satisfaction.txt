C. Other drivers of low scores
------------------------------
  - Avg score: single-item vs multi-item orders: 4.14 vs 3.61
  - Avg score: single-seller vs multi-seller orders: 4.13 vs 2.86
  - Multi-seller orders: count / late rate on paper / 1-star share: 1263 / 1.0% / 35.6%
  Hypothesis (not provable here): split parcels - the recorded delivery date likely reflects only one of the shipments, so these orders look on-time while customers wait for the rest.
  - Avg score: orders canceled / unavailable: 1.80 / 1.53
  - Avg score: delivered orders: 4.16
  - Avg score: 'shipped' orders (never confirmed delivered): 2.00
  - Lowest avg score among big states: MA (3.76)
  - Highest avg score among big states: PR (4.18)
