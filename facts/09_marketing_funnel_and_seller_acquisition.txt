E. Do won leads turn into selling sellers? (link funnel -> orders)
------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Closed leads that appear in the sellers table: 380 of 842 (45.1%)
  - ...of which made at least one valid sale: 379
  - GMV from those sellers: R$ 674,005 = 5.0% of all GMV
  - Median GMV per won seller (with sales): R$ 548
  - Median GMV per all other seller: R$ 917  (not like-for-like: won sellers joined later, so had less time to sell)
  - Won sellers whose first sale came AFTER the won date: 379 of 379
  - Median days from won date to first sale (those): 44
  NOTE: won_date goes to Nov 2018, orders end Oct 2018, so the funnel's later cohorts cannot be observed selling.
