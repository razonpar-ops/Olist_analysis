01 - DATASET OVERVIEW & DATA QUALITY
====================================
Source: olist.sqlite (Brazilian e-commerce marketplace 'Olist' + its seller-acquisition funnel).

B. Time coverage
----------------
  - First order: 2016-09-04 21:15:19
  - Last order: 2018-10-17 17:30:18
  - Months with < 1,000 orders (sparse / ramp-up / cut-off): 2016-09, 2016-10, 2016-12, 2017-01, 2018-09, 2018-10
  - Window used for trend analysis: Jan 2017 - Aug 2018 (20 full months)
  - Leads (MQL) first-contact range: 2017-06-14 to 2018-05-31
  - Leads closed (won) range: 2017-12-05 to 2018-11-14
  NOTE: 'won_date' runs past the last order date, i.e. the funnel tables and the order tables are NOT time-aligned.

  Reading guide:
  - order_reviews.review_comment_title/message: nulls are normal - most customers leave only a star rating.
  - orders.order_delivered_*: nulls are normal for orders that were never delivered (canceled, unavailable, in transit).

D. Duplicates
-------------
  - geolocation fully duplicated rows: 261,831 of 1,000,163 (26.2%)
  - geolocation distinct zip prefixes: 19,015
  - order_reviews: review_ids that appear on >1 row: 789
  - order_reviews: orders with >1 review: 547
  - customers: customer_id (per order) vs customer_unique_id (per person): 99,441 vs 96,096
  => Always count PEOPLE with customer_unique_id, not customer_id.
  - Other tables with fully duplicated rows: none

-------------------------------------------------

                          relationship  orphan rows

      order_items.seller_id -> sellers            0
     order_payments.order_id -> orders            0
      order_reviews.order_id -> orders            0

  - Orders with NO items: 775 (mostly unavailable/canceled)
  - Orders with NO payment record: 1
  - Orders with NO review: 768
  - Products whose category is not in translation table: 13 products / 2 categories (pc_gamer, portateis_cozinha_e_preparadores_de_alimentos) - mapped manually
  - Closed leads whose seller_id exists in sellers table: 380 of 842 (45.1%)
  - Customer zips found in geolocation: 99.72%
  - Seller zips found in geolocation: 99.77%
  => Core transactional tables join cleanly: no orphan keys anywhere.

F. Anomalies found
------------------
  - Orders handed to carrier BEFORE purchase timestamp: 166
  - Orders delivered to customer BEFORE carrier pickup date: 23
  - 'delivered' orders with no delivery date: 8
  - 'canceled' orders that still have a delivery date: 6
  - Reviews created BEFORE the order was placed: 74
  - Geolocation points outside Brazil's bounding box: 42
  - Orders where payments != items + freight (>1 cent): 303 of 98,665 (0.31%)
  - Payment rows with value = 0: 9
  - Payment type 'not_defined': 3
  - Order items with freight = 0: 383
  - Products with weight = 0 g: 4
  - Max single item price: R$ 6,735.00
  - Typos in column names (kept as-is): product_name_lenght, product_description_lenght
  - City names in geolocation: inconsistent accents/spelling ('sao paulo' vs 'são paulo') -> join on zip prefix, not on city

G. How the anomalies were handled in this analysis
--------------------------------------------------
  - Orders with negative stage durations are excluded only from the stage-by-stage timing chart; they stay in everything else.
  - Canceled/unavailable orders are excluded from revenue (GMV); all orders are included in status counts.
  - One review per order (the latest) so scores are not double counted.
  - Geolocation is averaged per zip prefix after removing points outside Brazil.
  - Customers are counted with customer_unique_id.
  - Trend charts use Jan 2017 - Aug 2018 only.
