07 - DELIVERY & LOGISTICS
=========================

A. Delivery performance (delivered orders)
------------------------------------------
  - Orders analysed: 96,470
  - Average purchase -> delivered: 12.6 days
  - Median: 10.2 days
  - 90th percentile: 23.1 days
  - 99th percentile: 46 days
  - Delivered within 7 days: 27.0%
  - Delivered within 14 days: 68.7%
  - Took longer than 30 days: 4.7%
  - Average ESTIMATED delivery time promised to customer: 23.7 days
  - Average days earlier than estimate: 11.9 days
  - Orders delivered LATE (after estimated day): 6,534 (6.8%)
  - Average lateness of late orders: 10.6 days
  - Late by more than 7 days: 3.0% of all delivered orders
  => Estimates are very conservative: on average customers receive goods ~12 days BEFORE the estimated date.
